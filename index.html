About

Behind the cut

I'm a video editor and producer with 8+ years of experience across the full production pipeline — shooting, editing, motion graphics, color, and delivery. At Meta Reality Labs, I work as the sole video production specialist embedded in R&D teams, owning projects from concept through final delivery.

Before that, I cut high-volume social content at Offbeat Media Group and worked broadcast production at QVC. I hold a BS in Film and Television from Drexel University.

Outside of client and studio work, I build personal 3D and motion projects in Blender — particle physics, cloth simulation, and character animation — and run smaller illustrated series like a regional Pokédex graphic set.
