About

Behind the cut

I'm a Multi Media Content Creator, blending creativity and technical precision to bring stories to life. For the past two years, I've worked at Meta Reality Labs as the sole Technical Artist on site, overseeing high-end production gear, filming and photographing on location, and editing hundreds of research videos that support the future of AR/VR.

Before joining Meta, I cut my teeth in fast-paced creative environments. At Offbeat Media Group, I edited 200+ Snapchat shorts that reached audiences in the hundreds of thousands, and at QVC, I contributed to campaigns for Candace Cameron Bure's fashion line, corporate training videos, and YouTube content with 300K+ views. I've also designed original posters for print and digital campaigns, merging my eye for design with strong storytelling.

I hold a B.S. in Film & Television from Drexel University, where I honed my skills in editing, cinematography, sound design, and VFX through hands-on projects and productions. That foundation, paired with 8+ years of professional editing experience, taught me how to balance creative vision with technical execution.

Fluent in the Adobe Creative Suite, DaVinci Resolve, Blender 3D, and advanced production workflows, I thrive on projects that require both speed and polish. Whether it's research content, branded campaigns, or imaginative short films, I bring energy, versatility, and detail to every frame.
